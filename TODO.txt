1. The "Net Caloric" Formula (Your Idea, Balanced)

This formulation tracks total calories burned, but ensures that actually eating something remains the ultimate priority.

    Prey: Fitness = timeLived + (energyGained * 2.0) - (energySpent * 1.0)

        Why: Prey need to survive above all else. This allows them to spend energy running away from predators without completely destroying their fitness score, but still punishes them for running in circles for no reason.

    Predator: Fitness = (energyGained * 2.0) - (energySpent * 1.0)

        Why: Predators are judged purely on hunting ROI (Return on Investment). Because energyGained is multiplied by 2, a successful hunt always yields a positive net score, but an efficient hunt yields a massive score.

2. The "Efficiency Ratio" (ROI)

Instead of subtracting, you divide. This measures the pure Return on Investment of the agent's actions.

    Both Species: Fitness = energyGained / (energySpent + 1.0) (The +1.0 prevents division by zero)

        Why it is great: A predator that spends 5 energy to get 33 energy (Score: 5.5) will beat a predator that spends 30 energy to get 66 energy (Score: 2.1), even though the second predator ate more prey. It breeds hyper-efficient assassins.

        The Catch: An agent that eats one blade of grass and then stands perfectly still for the rest of the generation will get a massive score.

3. The "Exploration vs Exploitation" Hybrid

If you want to ensure your agents don't just stand still, you can add an exploration metric. You can track the totalDistanceTraveled (by adding up their speed every tick).

    Prey: Fitness = timeLived + energyGained + (totalDistanceTraveled * 0.01) - energySpent

        Why: This explicitly rewards prey for exploring the map and finding new "Continents" of grass, while still punishing them if they burn too much energy doing it.
